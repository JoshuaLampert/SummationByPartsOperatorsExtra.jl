Changelog

SummationByPartsOperatorsExtra.jl follows the interpretation of semantic versioning (semver) used in the Julia ecosystem. Notable changes will be documented in this file for human readability.

Changes in the v0.1 lifecycle

Added

  • Allow Julia v1.10 (#94).

Changed

  • Multiplying a SubcellOperator with a vector (D * u and mul!(dest, D, u)) does not form the dense derivative matrix anymore. This avoids allocations and is about 19 times faster for 40 nodes (#118).
  • The right-hand side of MultidimensionalLinearAdvectionNonperiodicSemidiscretization does not allocate temporary vectors anymore and is about 7 times faster for 400 nodes (#119).
  • Invalid arguments to the operator constructors in the package extensions, e.g., missing regularization_functions for GlaubitzIskeLampertÖffner2026Regularized, now throw an ArgumentError instead of an AssertionError (#123).
  • function_space_operator and subcell_operator accept any AbstractVector of nodes, e.g., ranges or integer nodes, and subcell_operator accepts any real x_M. The collections passed as keyword arguments to subcell_operator are not modified anymore, and tuples are supported (#124).

Fixed

  • MultidimensionalLinearAdvectionNonperiodicSemidiscretization was not energy stable at corners adjacent to one inflow and one outflow boundary face, e.g., for tensor product operators and operators constructed with corners. The boundary data is now imposed at the nodes with τ_j < 0, where τ = diag(a[1] * B_1 + a[2] * B_2), which coincides with the previous rule at boundary nodes with a single normal (#112).
  • The weights of sub-cell operators constructed with subcell_operator were bounded by one, such that the operators were not exact (without any warning) if a sub-cell was longer than its number of nodes. The weights are now scaled by the length of the sub-cell. Note that this changes the meaning of the weight entries of a user-provided initial guess x0 (#113).
  • get_optimization_entries and get_multidimensional_optimization_entries returned infinite entries for weights equal to one and threw a DomainError for weights larger than one. The weights are now normalized before inverting the sigmoid function, which yields the same mass matrix in the optimization (#114).
  • The quantities energy_rate_boundary and energy_rate_boundary_dissipation computed by the AnalysisCallback for VariableLinearAdvectionNonperiodicSemidiscretization were wrong if the advection velocity was negative at the right boundary or had different signs at the two boundaries. The left and right boundaries are now treated independently.
  • Passing sparsity_patterns to subcell_operator always threw a MethodError (#116).
  • function_space_operator and subcell_operator sorted the passed vector of nodes in place. They now sort a copy and leave the passed vector unchanged (#117).
  • Passing basis_functions_weights different from one to multidimensional_function_space_operator led to wrong operators since the moments were not weighted consistently with the basis functions (#121).
